# UI design system notes

## One brand, two visual modes

Sovereign Nexus uses a single navy-led identity with two interface densities:

- **Editorial / public mode** uses generous whitespace, larger Manrope headlines, tonal surfaces, controlled asymmetry, and a Sydney professional-services tone.
- **Operational / workspace mode** uses a stable navy navigation rail, light working surfaces, compact metadata, and structural separators for efficient matter review.

## Color semantics

- Navy: Sovereign Nexus brand, institutional and legal authority, platform structure.
- Blue: neutral interaction and ordinary links.
- Electric purple: AI intelligence, Jurist Lens, AI-generated analysis and actions.
- Gold / amber: real human lawyer, VIP service, direct lawyer interaction.
- Red: risk, missing or urgent information, correction required.
- Green: verified, completed, or valid state.
- Neutral gray: metadata and inactive state.

These roles deliberately separate official law, AI synthesis, and lawyer advice.

## Surface before line

Public pages rely primarily on whitespace, background shifts, and tonal cards. Workspace screens allow subtle ghost separators when they improve parsing between columns, inputs, lists, and evidence structures. Strong decorative borders are avoided.

## Icon philosophy

Icons are functional: documents, folders, globe, calendar, people, shields, upload, search, and messages. Legal clichés are not used as the main visual language.

## Provenance styling

Policy detail pages use distinct sections for:

1. official source / statutory text,
2. AI synthesis / Jurist Lens,
3. impact and compliance interpretation,
4. human lawyer memo.

Each section carries a visible provenance label and a distinct tonal accent.

## Public vs workspace behavior

Public pages communicate the service model and route users into the matter. Workspace pages preserve the matter identity, facts, documents, authorities, and summary while changing only the active service actor: purple AI or gold human lawyer. On mobile, the workspace rail becomes a drawer and the matter panel stacks below the conversation.
